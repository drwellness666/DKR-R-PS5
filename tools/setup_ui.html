DKR-R PS5 · optional source build

Build your PS5 version

Select your two own US ROMs, let the assistant build the game, then send it to your PS5. Compilation happens on this PC. Once installed, the game runs natively on the PS5 without a running PC.

1 · Choose both ROMs

Select your clean US 1.0 and US 1.1 / Rev A dumps. Both are required; switch later under Touchpad → Play. Original files are only read. PAL dumps are unsupported.

No ROM selected.

2 · Build

The first build downloads public development tools and sources and can take a while. ROMs are never uploaded to GitHub. Keep this window and its terminal open while building. Completed builds are kept locally so you can resume later. Never share the generated ROM-containing installer.



3 · Install on your PS5
Start your console's ELF receiver and ShadowMountPlus first. This installs your local build and ROMs into DKR-R's own folders. For an update, choose the previous build you installed. Every old file is checked on the console before changes; ROMs, settings and saves are preserved. Unknown installations are refused.
Then scan /data/homebrew with ShadowMountPlus and start DKR-R from its PS5 icon. Once installed, play from the PS5 icon. No PC or separate save helper needs to stay running.
